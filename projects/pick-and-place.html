Servo-Actuated Gripper

CAD design and testing of a compact mechanical gripper for a pick-and-place robot designed for a 20 mm aluminum cube.

Project Overview

As part of a team project to develop a pick-and-place robot, I was responsible for designing the claw gripper. The gripper needed to integrate with a servo-driven mechanism and support handling of a 20 mm aluminum cube.

Contributions

  • Developed the gripper CAD model and assembled its mechanical components in SolidWorks.
  • Selected gear sizes and checked for interference between components.
  • Designed the linkage geometry and evaluated its fit through assembly in SolidWorks. 3D-printed the gripper components and assembled the physical mechanism for testing.
  • Tested the gripper mechanism to verify its operation.

Design and Testing

The design process involved developing the gripper geometry, checking component clearances, and assembling the mechanism in SolidWorks. Testing was used to evaluate the operation of the gripper.

Current Status

The gripper has been designed and tested successfully. Integration with the complete pick-and-place robot remains in development as the team project progresses.
