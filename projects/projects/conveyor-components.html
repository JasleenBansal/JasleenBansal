REVERSE ENGINEERING · INDUSTRIAL MECHANICAL DESIGN

Conveyor Shaft & Sprocket Assemblies

Recreated industrial mechanical components from physical parts, incorporating requested design modifications and producing dimensioned manufacturing drawings.

Project Overview

During an industrial maintenance placement, I worked on replacement and modified components for existing equipment. The work supported the replacement of worn or failed parts and the preparation of components for planned plant shutdown maintenance.

Since the original components were available physically, I took measurements directly from the parts and used them as the basis for CAD models and manufacturing drawings.

My Contributions

  • Measured existing physical components to capture the dimensions needed for CAD modelling.
  • Created component models in AutoCAD based on the measured geometry.
  • Incorporated requested design modifications to address component changes and replacement requirements.
  • Produced dimensioned manufacturing drawings to communicate component geometry and specifications.

Engineering Approach

The work followed a reverse-engineering process: measure the existing component, recreate its geometry in CAD, apply the requested modifications, and prepare dimensioned drawings. This translated physical components into engineering documentation that could support replacement and maintenance planning.

Application

The drawings supported maintenance preparation for components that had failed, were worn, or were being considered for modification ahead of scheduled shutdown periods. Accurate dimensions and clear drawings were important for communicating the intended component design.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Reverse engineering from physical components
  • Dimensional measurement and CAD modelling
  • Engineering drawing and dimensioning
  • Design modifications and maintenance support
  • Industrial engineering documentation
