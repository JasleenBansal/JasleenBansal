Card Dealing Robot

Project Gallery

Designed and fabricated structural components for a card-dealing robot using SolidWorks, 3D printing, AutoCAD, and laser cutting.

Project Overview

As part of a team project to develop a card-dealing robot, I contributed to the mechanical structure by designing components for the base and platform. The work involved preparing models and drawings for fabrication and integrating the manufactured parts into the robot assembly.

Contributions

  • Designed the robot base and its supporting legs in SolidWorks.
  • Created the CAD components for 3D printing and fabricated the parts using additive manufacturing.
  • Created an AutoCAD drawing of the acrylic plate supporting the robot body and laser cut.
  • Assembled the robot's manufactured components and integrated with the motor and bearings.

Design and Fabrication

The project involved using different CAD tools and manufacturing methods to produce components for the robot. SolidWorks was used to model the three-dimensional structural parts, which were then 3D-printed. AutoCAD was used for the acrylic rotating plate design for laser cutting.

Summary

  • 3D CAD modelling and component design
  • Additive manufacturing and laser cutting
  • Mechanical assembly and design for fabrication
