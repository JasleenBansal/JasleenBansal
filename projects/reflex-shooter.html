Reflex Shooter Game

Project Gallery

Designed and 3D-printed flywheels for a ping-pong ball launching mechanism, contributing to the mechanical assembly of a reflex game built by a team.

Project Overview

As part of a team project to develop a reflex-based shooting game, I contributed to the design and construction of the ball-launching mechanism. My primary responsibility was modelling the flywheels in SolidWorks and 3D printing, with dimensions selected to suit the size of the ping-pong balls used by the system.

Contributions

  • Modelled the flywheels in SolidWorks, accounting for the dimensions of the ping-pong balls used in the mechanism.
  • 3D-printed the flywheels for use in the physical prototype.
  • Contributed to constructing the base and shaft assembly supporting the mechanism.
  • Assembled the mechanical components into the overall prototype.

Design and Fabrication

The flywheels were designed in CAD and fabricated using 3D printing. The project also involved contributing to the supporting base and shaft assembly so the components could be integrated into the prototype.

Summary

  • SolidWorks part modelling
  • Design for additive manufacturing
  • Mechanical component integration
  • Prototype assembly
